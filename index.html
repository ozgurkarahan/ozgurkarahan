The ideas behind the work.

Three questions I bring to enterprise AI.

01 What can it change?

A task, not just an answer.

Horizon Famille: a customer calls about a booking. The agent checks the situation, quotes a change before acting, and routes a discount outside its mandate to a person in Teams.

  • Watch the call 6m19s ↗
  • Open the presentation ↗
  • Inspect the source ↗

Demonstration scope. A real recorded phone call through Voice Live and Azure Communication Services; mock CRM, ERP, and knowledge-base services; Power Platform approval in Teams. Not a production deployment.

02 Whose authority does it use?

The caller's identity matters.

An agent's connection to a business system should not erase the person making the request. Carrying an identity is only part of the job: authorization still needs to be enforced at each boundary.

  • Identity propagation, explained →
  • Customer 360 Assistant ↗
  • The article on identity in Salesforce meta-tools ↗

These samples are not audited production guarantees. Read the evaluation boundaries before adapting a pattern.

03 When should a person decide?

A capable agent also needs a stop line.

More autonomy is not automatically a better outcome. My starting point is to make the permitted action, the escalation condition, and the human decision explicit. Horizon's discount-approval workflow is one concrete example.
